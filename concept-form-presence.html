Principal artistic practices
01 · PERFORMANCE ART

The presence
of the body.

Performance Art brings together literature-art, visual-art, sound and music-art through the body, duration, space, audience and encounter. The work happens through presence.

02 · CINEMATIC ART

The presence
of the world.

Cinematic Art is the creative documenting of presence: people, places, gestures, situations, time and the act of looking. The camera becomes a way of encountering what is there.

The artistic question

What is it that becomes present when a concept finds its form?

The work begins with an idea, but the idea is not the artwork. It must encounter a material, a body, a space, an image, a sound, a duration or a situation. In that encounter, something unexpected can appear. That is the moment of rediscovery.

Another form of expression

Generic Multiform Electronic Media Art

A contemporary form available alongside the principal practices. Text, images, video, sound, computer-generated material and edited recordings can all become materials for expression. AI belongs here too: as one of the many tools available to the contemporary artist.

Not the destination.

The technology is not the artistic interest. The medium changes; the question remains.
