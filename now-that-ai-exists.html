Now That
AI Exists

"Art asks me to rediscover presence.
Media allows me to produce and circulate forms."

Things I want to make, investigate and attempt now that the cost of producing images, films, characters, research and ideas has radically changed.

Things newly possible beyond the channel

01

Books from Amizone

Turn ideas from Amizone into books and fiction, including experiments such as a fictional work around Shah Rukh Khan.

02

Cinema, Society & Culture

Continue scholarly work using AI as a research, comparison, synthesis and thinking partner.

03

My Critical Theories

Develop original theory books emerging from sessions, workshops and encounters at Conflictorium.

04

Vaufe Bag

A bag and accessory brand shaped with AI for design, visual language, product stories and circulating the collection.

05

Style Me App

An app that uses AI to suggest looks, outfits and styling from what someone already owns or wants to wear.

06

Study Abroad App

A guide through universities, applications, visas and life abroad, with AI helping compare options and draft the paperwork of leaving.

07

Learn English App

A speaking, reading and writing companion that uses AI to practise English with a person rather than a worksheet.

08

Learn to Sing App

A practice room in the phone: pitch, phrasing and songs, with AI listening back and helping someone learn to sing.

YouTube-based work made with AI

01

Our World

An AI-character YouTube universe with Arjun, the Infotainer; Safia, the Historian; Charu, learning science magic with Charu; and other recurring characters — including The Great Debate, a current-affairs and ideas format with AI characters representing different positions.

02

AI Versions of Songs

Reimagine existing songs through AI and explore a new YouTube format around musical transformation.

03

Animated Short Stories

Produce animated stories without requiring the conventional scale of an animation production team.

04

AI Cinema

Make films through AI characters and motion-capture / performance-driven workflows.

05

AI Science Classroom

A YouTube channel explaining science from Class 6 to Class 12 through AI-generated lessons and visualisations.

Without AI

01

Documentary Art Films

Films grounded in real people, real spaces, real encounters and the documentary act itself.

02

Performance Art

Works emerging from the body, presence, space, audience and live encounter.

03

Urdu Poetry

Writing and performing poetry where language, memory, voice and lived experience remain central.

What should I ask AI to make, what must I still make myself, and what becomes possible when the two meet?
